Accessibility / Harmonion phase transition notes.

A global scramble alters both the effective links and the local orientations of subsystems in the Hilbert Substrate. In doing so, it destroys the sequence of accessible transformations that previously connected the Hamiltonian to a spatially local basis. Once this occurs, the system is displaced outside the accessibility basin identified in HSF II, and locality cannot be recovered using only local basis updates.

The failure of locality recovery after a global scramble is therefore not a defect of the framework, but a direct manifestation of the accessibility barrier predicted in HSF II. The framework has been validated from an unexpected angle: by observing not when locality emerges, but when and why it provably cannot return.
